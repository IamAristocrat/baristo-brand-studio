# Baristo.Online — On-Demand Batch Website Release Checklist

Release objective: execute the 13-point packaging, roast-science and premium-positioning plan without describing the packaging as a temporary or inferior solution.

## 1. Site-wide packaging language — COMPLETE
Exact approved disclosure used site-wide:

> The imagery on this site represents the Baristo packaging design language. Current release batches are packed in premium pouches and finished with signature Baristo labels. Product quality, roast integrity, and brand specifications remain unchanged.

Implemented globally and repeated near primary packaging representations.

## 2. Protect premium perception of sticker-finished pouches — COMPLETE
The applied label is framed as a Signature Label Standard within an On-Demand Batch model. The site explicitly treats label proportion, alignment, clean edges, bubble-free application, consistent margins and barrier-pouch quality as controlled presentation variables.

## 3. Website content architecture — COMPLETE
Packaging representation, roast architecture, brewing intelligence, On-Demand Batch process and FAQ are separated into dedicated sections rather than being buried in product-card copy.

## 4. Section naming — COMPLETE
Primary technical section is named **Roast Architecture**. Supporting terminology includes **Target Roast Architecture**, **Brewing Intelligence**, **Signature Label Standard** and **On-Demand Batch**.

## 5. Universal scientific framework — COMPLETE
The site explains the variables widely used to characterise roast development: objective color, mass loss, post-first-crack development, surface condition, roast-curve behaviour, aroma/sensory evaluation and extraction behaviour. It explicitly rejects a single universal end-temperature or roast-time claim because measurement depends on roaster design and probe configuration.

## 6. Product-specific scientific roast content — COMPLETE
Noble Dark and Truly Dark each have detailed target architecture covering classification, color target, indicative Agtron-style orientation, indicative mass loss, development position, surface condition, body, acidity, bitterness, solubility, aromatic family, finish and method suitability.

## 7. Noble Dark vs Truly Dark comparison — COMPLETE
A responsive comparison table explains roast family, expression, body, acidity, bitterness, sweetness, surface appearance, solubility, cup mood, espresso use and milk pairing.

## 8. Representation-image strategy — COMPLETE
New vector representation assets were added for Noble Dark roast development, Truly Dark roast development and the On-Demand Batch signature-label system. All are explicitly labelled as scientific/design representations rather than exact production charts or exact delivered pack geometry.

## 9. Correct placement across the site — COMPLETE
Homepage: packaging disclosure after hero, Roast Architecture after the two-roast section, Brewing Intelligence after Ritual Lab, On-Demand Batch and FAQ before footer.

Roast product pages: local packaging disclosure, product-specific technical architecture, brewing intelligence and On-Demand Batch section.

Evidence Hub: packaging disclosure, measurement policy, both target roast architectures and research references.

All public routes: global packaging-representation note rendered through the root route.

## 10. Exact packaging FAQ — COMPLETE
FAQ includes: **Why may the delivered pouch look slightly different from the website image?** The answer uses the approved disclosure verbatim.

## 11. Scientific and cognitive claim discipline — COMPLETE
The Evidence Hub retains the rule that Baristo does not guarantee cognitive enhancement or physiological outcomes. Technical content describes roast, extraction and sensory architecture rather than therapeutic effects.

## 12. Sticker issue reframed as On-Demand Batch — COMPLETE
No 'first release' framing is used. The site positions current packaging as an **On-Demand Batch** system with deliberately limited production lots and signature labels. The label is treated as the intended pack face, not an apology or placeholder.

The site references SCA roast-color measurement context and peer-reviewed Arabica roasting research on roast degree, chemical/sensory change, heat and mass transfer, mass loss and objective color.
